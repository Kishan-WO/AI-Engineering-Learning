Regular Expressions

A regular expression, or regex, is a pattern for matching text.

\d matches a digit, \w matches a letter, digit or underscore, and \s matches whitespace.
+ means one or more, * means zero or more, and ? means optional.
^ matches the start of a line and $ matches the end.

In Python, the re module provides re.search, re.findall and re.sub. Compile a pattern once with re.compile if you use it many times.
