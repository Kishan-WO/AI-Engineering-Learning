The Solar System

The Solar System has eight planets that orbit the Sun. The four inner planets, Mercury, Venus, Earth and Mars, are small and rocky. The four outer planets, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune, are much larger and made mostly of gas and ice.

Jupiter is the largest planet. Venus is the hottest, because its thick atmosphere traps heat. Pluto was reclassified as a dwarf planet in 2006.
